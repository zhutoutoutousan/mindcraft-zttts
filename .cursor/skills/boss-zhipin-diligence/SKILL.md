# BOSS直聘 diligence

Many accounts on this site are thin shells or off-platform funnels. Treat a new thread as untrusted until the check below is done. Named results live in `.private/boss-zhipin-diligence.md` (gitignored). [reference.md](reference.md) only points there. Append new checks in that private file and in `.private/job-bewerbungen.fu.md`.

## Before any send, click, or profile edit

1. Read `.private/boss-zhipin-diligence.md`. If the account is already **stop**, do not message, do not complete WeChat, do not join a group, do not quote a rate, do not pay.
2. For a name not in that file: business registry (founding date, capital, legal person, scope, address type) plus whether the same pitch is copy-pasted on other boards. Chat tone is evidence.
3. Log company, role, channel, and sent/not-sent in `.private/job-bewerbungen.fu.md`. No phone, no WeChat id, no mailbox password, no IBAN.
4. File the JD in `jd-market` the same day, including a 驻外 card that is not greeted. Market notes stay in that skill.

## Outbound

Public line only, when a reply is actually warranted:

作为项目成员参加了德国大学的研究项目。目前在国内，找远程全职或兼职。

Human 2026-09-28: do not write 留学, a student identity, or 留学生. The Potsdam role stays an external-hire research project. Do not send a second bubble to walk back a line that already went out.

Do not mail the other person the agent's private rules. Never send: refusal of phone or 企业微信, a current city, a travel stay, "微信先不加", a skills confession (Linux years, "不是翻译"), or an apology about a previous bubble. Do not unsend. A leaked line stays; do not add a second bubble to explain it.

Roles that intersect the resume are in scope: frontend, fullstack, English teacher, German teacher, part-time. BOSS has no 德语教师 title; the bucket is **其他外语教师**. Do not add 服务员. Do not invent Laravel, Linux years, a translator title, or a rate.

## WeChat versus phone

- An open **交换微信** card (dialog type weixin): accept. Do not announce it.
- **交换联系方式** / 换电话 is phone. Do not accept it because they said 企业微信.
- If diligence says stop, a WeChat request already sent is not completed: no add, no group, no 「1」, no quote, no payment. Do not discuss that in the chat.

## Stop signs

Leave the platform, phone equals 企业微信, reply 「1」 in a group, quote a price to a project manager, pay for training, deposit, uniform, download an unknown app, register through a referral link, or send a user id. A residential address plus tiny capital plus a job the license does not match is a shell, not an employer.
