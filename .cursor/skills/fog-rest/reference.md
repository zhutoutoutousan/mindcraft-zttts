## Two classes of "head won't go"

| Class | What it is | What it is not | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Post-study sore (human name DSB) | After a stretch of intellectual work; treat as endurance SORE | Nature 2024 mouse CA1 enhancer breaks; not GenomicInstability | Stop DAY / lexicon; log endurance |
| Inflammatory fog / sickness behavior | Peripheral cytokines discount high-effort cognition; low-demand intake preferred | Not the mouse paper; not DOMS | Skip lexicon and PROBE until asked; rest, sleep, mucosa |

Sources: Dantzer sickness-behavior literature; Frontiers Aging Neuroscience 2023 peripheral inflammation and neurocognitive impairment; Nature 2024 s41586-024-07220-7 (mouse CA1 only).

## Sickness behavior (generic)

Hart 1988; Dantzer 2001; Dantzer *Nat Rev Neurosci* 2008 (nrn2297). This is a **motivational reorganization**, not a willpower failure.

**Cluster (often together, not all required):**

- Anorexia / less food-motivated behavior (Kent 1996)
- Lethargy, more sleep, less exploration
- Social withdrawal
- High-effort cognition discounted; low-demand intake preferred (short video, not lexicon)
- The same discount hits **sequential executive work**: packing, inventory, multi-step procedures. Starting that class can feel like impending illness or weakness. That is the motivational brake, not laziness.
- Sometimes fever, aching, lower pain threshold

**Why appetite drops:** IL-1β and TNF-α signal the brain (fast: afferents from the inflamed site, often vagus; slow: circumventricular organs / choroid plexus). The hypothalamus (including arcuate involvement in IL-1 anorexia, Reyes & Sawchenko 2002) shifts priorities away from foraging. In animals, “working for food” falls harder than taking free chow in some designs; anorexia and anhedonia can split (Merali et al. 2003). Adaptive reading: conserve energy and reduce exposure while the innate program runs. Not a weight-loss protocol.

**If it stays on:** the same literature says prolonged immune-to-brain signaling can look more like depression in vulnerable people. That is about duration, not a same-day diagnosis.

**Load:** skip high-effort study and sequential executive work (packing, inventory, multi-step procedures). Soft, non-acid food if mucosa is repairing. Do not invent fat loss from a few days of scale noise. Creatine still wants some food if they eat at all.

**Not this class:** post-study sore named DSB; Nature 2024 mouse CA1; DOMS; a planned cut.

## Auditory beats

- PLOS One 2023 (Ingendoh et al.): EEG effects of binaural beats are inconsistent. Entrainment is not a reliable mechanism claim.
- Anxiety adjunct: mixed systematic reviews; small, heterogeneous RCTs (e.g. Appl. Sci. 2024).
- Pain: BMC Complement Med Ther 2024; low-to-very-low GRADE; alpha or delta-to-alpha sometimes for acute pain, not chronic fog.
- Klichowski et al. 2023 (large home sample): 15 Hz binaural beats *worsened* intelligence-test scores. Do not use beats as a study overlay.
- Longitudinal habituation: reviews note almost no trials >60 days. Fade is treated as novelty/placebo decay plus sensory adaptation, not receptor "tolerance" with a published curve.
- Hemi-Sync / commercial "alpha tracks": nested beats + noise + voice. Independent RCTs sparse. Treat as the same class as binaural rest audio.

Protocol class (not personal clocks):

- Stereo headphones required for binaural difference.
- 12–20 min, not hours.
- Rest window: after a crash or 30–60 min before sleep. Not as a morning stimulant. Not stacked on a large caffeine sitting.
- Target difference ~8–10 Hz (alpha) or ~4–6 Hz (theta). Avoid beta/gamma and 15 Hz when cognition is the goal.
- Cycle: 5 days on / 2 off, or ~3 weeks on / 1 week off. Off days: sit, same lights, silence or untreated noise. Change carrier, do not chase louder beats.
- The sit/no-screen ritual is the part with a plausible active ingredient.

## Caffeine class

EFSA 2015 (healthy adults, general population):

- Single sitting: up to 200 mg does not raise safety concerns.
- Daily from all sources: up to 400 mg (~5.7 mg/kg for a 70 kg reference adult) does not raise safety concerns.
- 100 mg near bedtime may change sleep in some adults.
- Mean half-life ~5 h (wide individual range).

Subjective "feels fine" at several times that amount is adenosine-receptor upregulation, not a new safety ceiling. Sleep architecture damage is the main fog amplifier.

L-theanine 100–200 mg is studied with *moderate* caffeine (often ~1:1 or more theanine than caffeine), not with 4× EFSA sittings.

## Other classes

| Class | Typical evidence role | Fog / mucosa notes |
|---|---|---|
| Creatine monohydrate 3–5 g/day | Brain phosphocreatine; more data in sleep loss / vegetarians / older adults than in healthy well-slept people | Keep as cheap, well-studied food-like compound |
| Magnesium glycinate | Moderate for fatigue/attention if deficient. Sleep trials small. Elemental Mg is ~14% of bisglycinate salt weight unless the label says elemental | Dose from **elemental** mg. Common trial-ish range 200–350 mg elemental/day |
| Multivitamin | Benefit mostly in deficiency. Chronic high B6 neuropathy risk | Check stacked preformed vitamin A against UL |
| Cod liver oil | Constraint is vitamin A, not EPA/DHA. Adult preformed vitamin A UL ~3000 µg RAE/day. Multivitamin + liver oil stacks retinol | Prefer reading EPA+DHA mg; one retinol source at a time |
| EPA/DHA (fish body oil) | Modest attention/processing over weeks; anti-inflammatory diet class | Different product from liver oil |
| Ceylon cinnamon | Lower coumarin than cassia; glycemic side-car | Not a fog RCT |
| Probiotics | Strain-specific | Space ≥3 h from non-selective antimicrobials |
| Berberine | Metabolic / AMPK; GI and CYP3A4 | Not first-line for acute fog |
| Oregano oil (carvacrol) | Non-selective antimicrobial; mucosa irritant; not for months | Pause during mucosa repair; not with probiotics in the same hour |
| Neem extract | Sparse human cognitive data; liver / reproductive animal and case concerns | Pause unless a clinician named it |
| Apple cider vinegar caps / betaine HCl | Acid. Betaine HCl is contraindicated in peptic ulcer disease | Pause during oral mucosa repair |
| Melatonin | Timing hormone, not a sedative. Physiological range often 0.3–1 mg. Higher OTC doses raise next-day grogginess without better sleep in several dose-finding lines | Do not fight a large caffeine sitting with more melatonin |

Not a diagnosis. Fever, stiff neck, new focal neuro signs: clinic, not this table.
