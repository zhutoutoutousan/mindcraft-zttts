## Load rule

If the human names inflammatory fog, resistance to heavy thinking, a short appetite dip, or that hard thought / packing / inventory / other multi-step procedures feel like impending illness: treat the cluster as sickness behavior. Skip Wordschatz, PROBE, and complex sequencing until they ask. Short low-demand intake is allowed. Do not force a full meal plan. Post-study sore named DSB is a different stop in endurance.

**Agent stream:** while this cluster is on, do not open long media megabatches (multi-`GenerateImage`, multi-gallery `WebFetch`, encode+Fetch in one turn) unless the human names the deliverable. Prefer local scripts and small turns — `.cursor/skills/cursor-agent-retry` Agent-side load.

## Audio (generic)

Stereo headphones. Rest ritual, not a study aid. 12–20 min. Alpha ~8–10 Hz or theta ~4–6 Hz. Not during tests or lexicon (15 Hz has worsened test scores in a large home sample). Cycle 5 on / 2 off or 3 weeks on / 1 week off. Do not raise "dose" when the effect fades. Details in reference.md.

## Supplements (generic)

Apply class rules from reference.md to the private stack. Not a prescription. Mucosa-repair weeks: pause acids and non-selective antimicrobials. Caffeine class limit is EFSA 400 mg/day and 200 mg/sitting for the general adult population; subjective tolerance is not that limit.
