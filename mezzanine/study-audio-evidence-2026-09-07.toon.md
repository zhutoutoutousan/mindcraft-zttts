## What the evidence actually says

| Finding | Grade / note | Source class |
|---|---|---|
| Background music overall: small positive *d*≈0.31 on learning outcomes (heterogeneous) | Meta | de la Mora Velasco & Hirumi, *Psychology of Music* 2023 |
| Lyrics hurt verbal memory, visual memory, reading (*d*≈−0.3) | Exp | Souza & Barbosa, *Journal of Cognition* 2023 |
| Instrumental lo-fi: **not better than silence** on performance; milder than lyrics | Exp | same JoC 2023 |
| Lo-fi vs classical vs silence: no significant exam difference (pilot) | Pilot | Kwantlen Psych Student Journal |
| "Mozart effect" = short arousal/mood, not general intelligence | Meta tradition | Rauscher 1993 → later meta as enjoyment/arousal |
| 15 Hz binaural **worsened** intelligence-test scores (large home sample) | Exp | Klichowski et al. 2023 |
| Binaural entrainment unreliable | Review | Ingendoh et al. PLOS One 2023 |
| White/pink noise: small benefit mainly in ADHD/high-attention-problem samples; may slightly hurt typical controls | Meta | Nigg et al. JAACAP 2024 class |
| Café speech is the real enemy for reading/writing | Mechanism | irrelevant speech effect / phonological loop |

## Practical rule for café learning (today)

1. **No lyrics** if reading, writing, vocab, interview banks.
2. Prefer **instrumental lo-fi / soft classical / coloured noise** as *mask + mood*, not as a score booster.
3. Volume low; if you notice the melody, turn down.
4. Hard PROBE / timed test → silence or soft pink/brown only.
5. Skip binaural-as-study-overlay (especially beta/15 Hz). Keep binaural for rest sits.
