term: time structure
de: Zeitstruktur / Tagesstruktur
gloss: perceived use of time as structured and purposive
not: willpower failure; DSB; bipolar label from IPSRT papers

core:
  Bond & Feather 1988 JPSP 55(2) 321-329. Time Structure Questionnaire. "the degree to which individuals perceive their use of time to be structured and purposive." Factors include structured routine and sense of purpose — the two faces the human named.
  Jahoda 1981/1982 latent deprivation. Time structure is a latent function of employment: days/weeks/years given an external rhythm. When that drops, discretionary time can feel like a "tragic gift" (chaotic, squandered days) rather than free progress toward goals.

clock_sibling:
  Monk TH et al. 1991 Psychiatry Research 36:195-207 Social Rhythm Metric.
  Monk TH et al. 2002 J Sleep Res 11:183-190 "A simple way to measure daily lifestyle regularity."
  Hits = wake / first contact / start of work-like activity / dinner / bed within ~45 min of the habitual time. Daily going-out that slides those clocks is this measure, not the TSQ purpose items.

adjacent:
  Wilcock occupational imbalance — too much or too little occupation relative to rest/work/play needs; play can crowd valued work.
  Gollwitzer implementation intentions — if-then plans; a later tool if a routine block is designed. Not the capture term.
